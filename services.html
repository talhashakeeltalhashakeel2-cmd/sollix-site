Our Services

Fire Pump Systems

Reliable fire pump installations that deliver the water pressure and flow needed to fight fires effectively, fully compliant with DCD standards.

Firefighting Sprinkler Systems

Automatic sprinkler systems designed to detect and suppress fires in their earliest stages, protecting lives and minimizing property damage.

Fire Hose Reel Systems

Manually operated fire hose reels providing a first line of defense for occupants before fire services arrive.

FM200 Clean Agent Suppression Systems

Clean agent fire suppression ideal for server rooms and sensitive equipment, extinguishing fires without leaving residue.

LPG Tank & Piping Systems

Safe design and installation of LPG storage and piping systems, engineered to prevent leaks and reduce fire risk.

All Types of Firefighting Equipment Installation

Supply and installation of extinguishers, alarms, and other fire safety equipment tailored to your facility's needs.

Pre-Action Fire Protection Systems

Advanced two-stage suppression systems that prevent accidental water discharge, ideal for areas with sensitive equipment or valuable assets.

Kitchen Hood Fire Suppression Systems

Automatic suppression systems for commercial kitchens, designed to detect and extinguish grease fires quickly and safely.
